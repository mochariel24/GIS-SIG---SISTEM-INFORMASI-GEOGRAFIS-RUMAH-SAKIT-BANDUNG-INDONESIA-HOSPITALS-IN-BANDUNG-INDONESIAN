A web-based GIS application for searching and viewing information on 42 hospitals in the city of Bandung—including their category, address, class, and coordinates—with the ability to open their locations and get directions via Google Maps.
Aplikasi web GIS untuk mencari dan melihat informasi 42 rumah sakit di Kota Bandung, termasuk kategori, alamat, kelas, dan koordinat, serta membuka lokasi dan petunjuk arah melalui Google Maps.
